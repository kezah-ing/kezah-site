# How to update your website

You never need to touch code. Everything on your site is edited in one place, the **site editor**.

**Open it:** go to your website address and add `/keystatic` at the end, for example `kezahkayitesi.com/keystatic`. Sign in when asked. You will see a dashboard with a list on the left: Case studies and decks, Articles, Logos, Testimonials, Offers, and Site settings.

**How saving works:** when you press **Save**, the change goes live on its own in about one to two minutes. Nothing else to do. Refresh your website after a couple of minutes to see it.

---

## Publish a new article, essay or poem

1. In the editor, click **Articles, essays and poems**, then the **+** button.
2. **Title:** type the title. The web address makes itself.
3. **Date:** pick the date it is published.
4. **Type:** choose Article, Essay or Poem. **Series** is optional (for example "The Rim Years").
5. **One-line gist:** one or two sentences that make someone want to read it.
6. Two ways to publish the text:
   - **Write it here:** paste the text into **Text**. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. The site gives it its own page.
   - **Link to it elsewhere:** paste the link (for example your Google Doc) into **Link to read it elsewhere** and leave Text empty.
7. Press **Create**. It appears in the "What I Think" section, newest first.

To hide a piece without deleting it, untick **Show on the site** and save.

## Add a case study and its deck

1. Click **Case studies and decks**, then **+**.
2. **Project name**, **Sector** (this feeds the filter buttons, so reuse a sector that already exists unless it is genuinely new), **Geography**, and the **One-line summary** shown on the card.
3. **Status, stated exactly.** Use the exact wording, for example "Submitted for ministerial review" or "Proof of concept proposed". Never upgrade it.
4. **Deck cover image:** upload the first slide as a picture (16 by 9). If you leave it empty, the site shows a tidy placeholder with the project name.
5. **Deck style:** pick the closest look (Editorial research, Dark green technical, Navy and gold). This only adds a small label under the cover.
6. Give the deck in one of two ways, or both:
   - **Deck PDF (upload):** the page then shows the deck to read and a download button.
   - **Canva share link:** the page then shows a "Read the deck" button that opens it.
7. Fill in **Three headline numbers** and the story fields (concept, insight, hypothesis, validation, evidence, lessons). Any field you leave empty simply does not appear.
8. **Order on the page:** 1 comes first. **Show on the home page** must be ticked for it to appear.
9. Press **Create**. The card and its own page appear together.

## Swap a logo

1. Click **Logos**, then open the organisation (or **+** for a new one).
2. Upload the new file in **Logo file**. A transparent PNG or SVG works best.
3. Save. Untick **Show on the site** to hide one.

The logo strip only appears once at least one logo is switched on.

## Add a testimonial

Click **Testimonials**, then **+**. Paste the quote, the person's name, role and organisation, and save. The quote section appears as soon as one is switched on.

## Edit an offer

Click **Offers**, open the one to change, edit the words and save. Use **Order** to move one earlier or later.

## Change the words on the home page, your bio, contact details or the booking text

Click **Site settings**. Everything on the home page that is not a case study, article, offer, logo or testimonial lives here: the headline, the intro, the bio, the numbers, the method stages, the "why" lines, your email and phone, and the booking calendar link.

## Add your one-page bio (PDF)

In **Site settings**, find **One-page bio (PDF)**, upload the file, and save. The "Download the one-page bio" button appears on its own. Upload a new file to replace it.

## Turn on the enquiry form

In **Site settings**, paste your Formspree form address into **Enquiry form address**. Until then, the form opens your email app with the message filled in.

## If something looks wrong

- **Wait two minutes and refresh.** Changes are not instant.
- **A section vanished.** Sections hide themselves when they are empty. Check you did not untick "Show on the site" and that the entry has its required fields.
- **A picture looks stretched.** Case-study covers should be 16 by 9. Re-upload a picture in that shape.
- **A change did not appear after ten minutes.** Open your Vercel dashboard, click your project, then **Deployments**. If the top one says Error, click it and send me a screenshot.
- **You made a mistake.** Open the entry, set it back, and save. Every save is also kept in your GitHub history, so I can restore an older version for you.
- **Anything else:** send me a screenshot and what you expected to see.
